Owala FreeSip vs Stanley Quencher

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Both are on the Home & Kitchen bestseller list. Pick by how you carry water, not by which color is posted this week.

Owala 24 oz wins if the bottle has to fit a cup holder and a bag. The lid locks. You can sip or gulp.

Stanley 40 oz wins if you sit at a desk or in a car with space and you want ice to last. The handle helps. The height does not help in a short cup holder.

Wash lids. That is where both get sour. If you only buy one, buy the size you will put in the vehicle tomorrow morning.
