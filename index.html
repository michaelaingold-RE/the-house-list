The three layers

First night

Sleep, shower, and not be visible from the street. Air mattress with a pump already in it. Paper shades you cut with kitchen scissors. Toilet paper, bags, a liner, a bulb.

First week

The house has to function while boxes are still closed. Hangers, stain spray, batteries, the ant bait or the fruit-fly trap — whichever month you closed.

First guests

Someone will show up before you are ready. Spare pillows, a second set of sheets, coffee that does not come out of a hotel packet.
