Duracell 2032 batteries

A 2032 is the coin cell in a lot of key fobs, scales, and trackers. Duracell’s 4-pack keeps circling the Health & Household bestsellers. Leave them in the pack until you need them.

When you change a fob battery, change it over a towel. The tiny gasket likes to vanish. If a device takes two cells, replace both.

Keep these away from kids. Coin cells are a swallowing hazard.
