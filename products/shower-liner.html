Barossa shower curtain liner

A clear liner is the piece people forget when they move. This one stays on the Home & Kitchen list because it is cheap, it has a weighted hem, and you can see through it if you also hang a fabric curtain.

Hook it so the magnets kiss the tub. Pull it closed after a shower so it dries. When it films over, throw it away. Liners are consumable.

Measure length. A standard 72-inch liner is wrong for a tall stall.
