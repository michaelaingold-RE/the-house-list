Liquid I.V. Lemon Lime

Health & Household · electrolyte mix

Liquid I.V. is the version of an electrolyte packet most people will actually finish. Lemon Lime is the flavor that keeps landing on the category bestseller list. It uses sugar. That is the trade for taste.

Take this on travel days, after a hot afternoon outside, or when someone in the house is off their food and still needs fluids. Skip it if you are avoiding sugar on purpose. LMNT is the other door.

One stick in about 16 ounces of water is the usual mix. It dissolves better in cool water than ice-cold water.
