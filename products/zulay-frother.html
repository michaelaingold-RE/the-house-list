Zulay handheld milk frother

This is a small battery wand. It froths milk. It also mixes protein powder so you do not end up with a sink full of clumps. That second job is why a lot of kitchens keep it.

It is not a cappuccino machine. Heat the milk first if you want hot foam. Rinse the whisk right away.

If you make one coffee a week, skip it. If you make one every morning, it is a reasonable $12.
