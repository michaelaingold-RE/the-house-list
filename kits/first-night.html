First night

Sleep before the truck arrives

Volume in this category sits on raised queen air beds with the pump built in. A separate pump is how you discover the outlet is on the other wall at 11 p.m. Intex’s high-rise Dura-Beam queen is the one that keeps showing up at the top of the sales pile — about a twenty-inch rise, pump in the unit, not a camping pad you unroll on cold subfloor.

Get queen unless you know you are alone. Twin is cheaper and you will regret it if two people close together. Put a cheap protector on it anyway. Air beds sweat.

Cover the windows before you “dress” them

Real shades wait until you live in the light for a week. Night one needs privacy you can cut with scissors and take down without holes. Two things sell hard here: stick-on pleated paper shades (Redi Shade and the no-drill cut-to-size packs) and static-cling frost film for baths and street-facing sliders.

Paper shades for bedrooms. Film for glass that faces a sidewalk. Blackout paper if you close in winter and the streetlight is in the room. Measure the glass, not the trim. Buy a multi-pack. One window is never the only window.

The unglamorous rest of night one

A weighted shower liner so the first shower does not flood the bath. A queen protector and a cheap sheet set so the air bed is not bare vinyl. Tall kitchen bags. A short pack of toilet paper. Soft-white LED bulbs — empty houses are sold with half the lamps missing.

If they close Nov – Feb

Buy blackout paper shades, not light-filter. Add a second set of cheap sheets. Dark comes early and the guest who “just wants to see the house” stays.

If they close Mar – May

Film on the slider first. Ant bait belongs in week one, but the first warm night is when the line shows up. Do not wait for the picnic.

If they close Jun – Aug

Raised bed, not a 10-inch pad on hot slab. A bottle that actually holds ice. Fruit-fly trap if produce is already in the kitchen.

If they close Sep – Oct

Same first-night stack. Add hangers before work clothes come out of garbage bags on Sunday night.
