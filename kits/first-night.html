First night

The truck is “on the way.” That sentence means the bed is not in the house. Neighbors can see in. There is no shower curtain and nothing on the towel bar.

Sleep before the truck arrives

Volume sits on raised queen air beds with the pump built in. Intex’s high-rise Dura-Beam queen keeps landing at the top of that pile. Twin is cheaper. Two people will hate you for it. Put a protector on it. Air beds sweat.

Cover the windows before you dress them

Paper shades you cut with scissors. Frost film on sliders that face a sidewalk. Measure the glass, not the trim. Multi-pack. One window is never the only window.

Shower, then something to dry off with

Weighted liner so the first shower does not flood the bath. Then a four-pack of cotton bath towels — Utopia and Tens are the packs that already move by the thousands. Not a spa set. Two for tonight, two for the person who shows up unannounced.

The rest of night one

Protector and a cheap sheet set. Tall kitchen bags. Toilet paper. Soft-white bulbs. Paper plates if first dinner is the floor picnic.
